The following combat and skill talents were designed for use in conjunction with the aethership rules.

++ Artifice Sphere Talents

++++ Emergency Shipwright [utility]
You may use one of your associated skills in place of Disable Device to perform Aethership Repair and divert power in an aethership, attempting both checks as if you were trained in Disable Device. You may perform battle fixes to an aethership as a standard action rather than a full-round action and may spend a use of skill leverage to reinforce the hull of a ship as a standard action rather than in 2 rounds.

If you possess the Field Repair talent, you may add the temporary hit points which would be granted from a battle fix to the temporary hit points granted by Field Repair when using the Field Repair talent on an aethership.

++++ Expert Engineer [utility]
While in an Engineer role on an aethership, you may use the divert power option as a full-round action, or a standard action if you are in an engineering bay module. Additionally, when you divert power to another part of your aethership, you grant additional effects:
//Weapons:// Grant a bonus on mounted aethership weapon damage equal to your operative modifier.
//Propulsion:// Grant a bonus on all Fly checks attempted by creatures in the Pilot role equal to your operative modifier.
//Aetheric Field:// Grant the aethership DR/— equal to your operative modifier.
//Defense:// Increase the aethership’s maximum Dexterity modifier by an amount equal to your operative modifier. 
**Associated Feat:** Chief Engineer.

++ Athletics Sphere Talents

++++ Ace Pilot
**Addendum:** For the purposes of this feat, you may treat aetherships as if they were vehicles and may use your choice of ranks in Fly or Profession (pilot) when attempting either type of check as the aethership’s pilot.

++++ Close-Quarters Starfighter [utility]
While in the Pilot role of an aethership, you may use your practitioner modifier in place of your Dexterity Modifier when calculating the aethership’s ACMB and ACMD. In addition, you may add any bonuses to your CMB and CMD from feats, talents, items, or class features to your aethership’s ACMB and ACMD.

++++ Hotshot Pilot [utility]
You gain access to several new maneuvers when in the Pilot role of an aethership (see **Table: Aethership Mastery** for details on specific maneuvers)
**Associated Feat:** Aethership Mastery

++ Communication Sphere Talents

++++ Captain’s Command [utility]
While you are filling the Copilot role on an aethership and using the aid another action as a standard action, you may use aid another on an additional ally taking an action as part of an aethership role plus an additional ally for every 4 ranks in an associated skill. You may use your Diplomacy modifier in place of your ranks in any other skill when using aid another in this way, rolling a single check for all allies. By spending a point of skill leverage, you may allow all allies benefited by this aid another check to roll twice and take the better result on the next attack roll or skill check they make as part of an aethership role before the start of your next turn.

++ Infiltration Sphere Talents

++++ Jamming Expert [utility]
Whenever you attempt the Run Interference action on an aethership, you may impose a penalty on two separate rolls rather than just one. By spending a point of skill leverage, you may instead impose a penalty equal to five times your ranks in Disable Device divided among any number of rolls any way you wish.

++++ Obscure Signal [utility]
While you are filling the Tactical role on an aethership, whenever you provide a dodge bonus to your aethership’s AC, you provide a bonus to the Knowledge DC attempted to scan your ship equal to your ranks in Disable Device. In addition, you may spend a point of skill leverage to provide a dodge bonus to your aethership’s AC as a free action rather than a move action.

++ Navigation Sphere Talents

++++ Tight Maneuvering [utility]
While you are filling the Pilot, Co-Pilot, or Tactical role on an aethership, the cost of movement for the first turn or maneuver the aethership performs each round is reduced by 5 feet or 1 space square (to a minimum of 0). By spending a use of skill leverage, you may allow your aethership to take any number of turns or maneuvers without spending movement and to move without provoking attacks of opportunity for 1 round.

++ Study Sphere Talents

++++ Lock-On Expertise [utility]
While you are filling the Tactical role on an aethership, for every 5 by which any of your Knowledge checks to scan exceeds the DC, any miss chance the target possesses is reduced by 10% rather than 5%. You may scan any ship you have previously scanned as a move action or as a free action by spending a use of skill leverage. When you spend skill leverage in this way, the target’s miss chance is reduced to 0%.

++++ Tactical Officer [utility]
While you are filling the Tactical role on an aethership, whenever you successfully scan an aethership, attacks made with your aethership’s weapons against the target take no penalty for targeting specific external modules (rather than a -5 penalty) and double their critical threat range (as the keen weapon special ability) for 1 round. Whenever you successfully scan an aethership, you may spend 1 use of skill leverage to grant you both the AC bonus and the attack bonus from the Tactical role for 1 round, but only against ships you have scanned this round.

++ Vocation Sphere Talents

++++ Chief Gunner [utility]
While you are filling the Weapons role on an aethership, you may operate two mounted weapons on an aethership rather than one, although you take a -4 penalty to attack rolls when doing so.

At the start of your turn during aethership combat, you may spend skill leverage to allow any crew and passengers operating aethership weapons to make attacks on your initiative count this round rather than their own.

When you or another crew member or passenger threatens a critical hit while targeting an aethership module with an aethership weapon, you may spend skill leverage to automatically confirm the critical hit.

++++ Chief Mate [utility]
While you are filling the Copilot role on an aethership, you may spend a move action to aid another on any ally taking an action as part of an aethership role. You may use your Profession (sailor) modifier in place of your ranks in any other skill when using aid another in this way. By spending a point of skill leverage as a standard action, you may grant a move or standard action to any other ally who is filling a role on an aethership.

++++ Star Pilot (trade) [utility]
You gain Acrobatics, Fly, Knowledge (engineering), and Knowledge (geography) as class skills. You may use the better of your Dexterity or Wisdom modifier for Fly checks and for determining the ACMB of any aethership you are piloting.
Associated Feat: Pilot’s Intuition
