Among the most volatile and dangerous warpriests are those who channel their god’s power through raw hatred, pledging to cleanse the world of differing belief systems by coercion or force.

This archetype requires the [[[champion warpriest]]] archetype and is subject to GM discretion regarding its appropriateness at the game table. This archetype deals concretely with religious intolerance and fellow players should be consulted as to whether they are comfortable exploring such topics through a mechanical lens.

++ ##4B0092|Trade Tradition##
If this is the character’s first level, the beacon of intolerance chooses a trade tradition. His trade rank is competent. If this is not the character’s first level, he instead chooses one trade talent from the Vocation sphere as a bonus talent. 

This replaces the warpriest’s normal class skills.

++ ##4B0092|Trophies of the Infidel##
At 1st level, the beacon of intolerance gains the Survivalism sphere as a bonus sphere, selecting the harvest package and the iconoclast drawback.

This replaces sacred talent.

++ ##4B0092|Militant Extremist##
A beacon of intolerance counts his class levels as fighter levels for the purpose of qualifying for feats that have a minimum number of fighter levels as a prerequisite and may use his class level as his base attack bonus to determine what combat feats he qualifies for. This stacks with other sources of base attack bonus.

++ ##4B0092|Swordpoint Conversion (Su)##
Whenever a beacon of intolerance reduces an intelligent enemy with Hit Dice equal to or less than his class level to less than 1 hit point or performs a successful coup de grace on it, he may attempt to purify it as part of the attack.

The target must attempt a Will save vs. a DC equal to 10 + 1/2 his beacon of intolerance level + his casting ability modifier. If the target fails, the impurities of its spirit are cleaved away, leaving behind a new believer. Its alignment changes to match the beacon of intolerance. This does not ensure any sort of allegiance to the beacon of intolerance, however, and does not even guarantee that it will not fight the beacon of intolerance for ideological reasons. If the creature succeeds, it is immune to the beacon of intolerance’s ability for 24 hours.

This effect can be reversed using a ritual equivalent of the atonement spell or any sphere ability that can remove permanent negative levels. This ability does not affect creatures without souls (like most undead), non-native outsiders, creatures with alignment descriptors, or creatures that are neutral because they lack the intelligence to be otherwise. If the target fails its save, the beacon of intolerance may choose to cancel the damage from the initiating attack.

+ Alternate Fervor and Channeling options
In addition to using Fervor to deal or heal damage on a creature, a warpriest may choose to gain the divine touch class feature of an avowed paladin, using his warpriest level in place of his paladin level. If he does this, he gains channel divinity at 4th level rather than the warpriest’s normal normal channel energy class feature (though channel divinity may be replaced as if it were channel energy). Uses divine touch gained in this way may be spent as if they were uses of fervor for the purpose of warpriest class features.

+ Favored Enemy Equivalent
At GM discretion, the Know the Infidel class creature of the Divine Champion archetype may count as the favored enemy class feature for the purpose of feats and prerequisites.
