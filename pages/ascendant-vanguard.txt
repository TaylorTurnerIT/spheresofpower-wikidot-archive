The battle for a planet’s future is fought on many fronts by many peoples, each of whom have their own reasons for seeking greater resolution. The most devoted and knowledgeable of these warriors are known as ascendant vanguards, fighting not only to defeat the unquestioned sovereignty of the current zeitgeist but also to establish the values and ideals which will guide the world into the future.

**Alignment:** Any

**Hit Die:** d8.

**Class Skills:** The ascendant vanguard’s class skills are Appraise (Int), Diplomacy (Cha), Disguise (Cha), Intimidate (Cha), Knowledge (all) (Int), Linguistics (Int), Perception (Wis), Perform (any) (Cha), Sense Motive (Wis), Spellcraft (Int), Stealth (Dex), Swim (Wis), and Use Magic Device (Cha).

**Skill Points at each Level:** 6 + Int modifier.

**Requirements**
To qualify to become an ascendant vanguard, a character must fulfill all of the following criteria.

* **Feats:** Dedicate Of Ascension.
* **Skills:** Knowledge (history) 7 ranks, Sense Motive 7 ranks.
* **Special:** Either any Squadron feat or the ability to share teamwork feats.

++ Weapon and Armor Proficiency
Ascendant vanguards gain no proficiency with any weapon or armor.

++ Ambition’s Reprieve (Ex)
At 1st level, the ascendant vanguard’s soul is placed beyond the reach of any deity or their agents. The ascendant vanguard does not go to any afterlife which is controlled by a deity, their soul taking on a form coinciding with their path of ascension. The character cannot be reanimated as undead or resurrected against their will, but may do either normally if they so wish.

++ Blended Talents/Spells Per Day
When a new ascendant vanguard level beyond 1st is gained, the character gains an increased caster level and talent-based ability progression (new combat, magic, skill, or blended talents, spell point progression, etc.) as if they had also gained a level in any one class they added before the prestige class level. Alternatively, they may gain new spells per day, spells known, and an increased caster level as if they had also gained a level in a spellcasting class they belonged to before taking the prestige class. They do not, however, gain any other benefit a character with that class would have gained (such as non-casting class abilities). If a character had more than one class before they became an ascendant vanguard, they must decide which class they add each level of ascendant vanguard to for the purpose of determining casting and talent progression.

++ Unified in Agency (Su)
At 1st level, the ascendant vanguard can tap into wells of knowledge and experience beyond the known world, endowing themself and their allies with the power to better shape their future. The ascendant vanguard gains a +1 sacred or profane bonus (chosen at the time this ability is gained) to all Int-, Wis-, and Cha-based skill checks. This bonus increases to +2 at 7th level.

At 4th level, the ascendant vanguard gains a +1 sacred or profane bonus on all Will saves. This bonus increases to +2 at 10th level.
Bonuses from unified in agency are automatically shared with any allies in the ascendant vanguard’s squadron or who are benefiting from an effect which allows the ascendant vanguard to share teamwork feats with their allies.

++ Conviction’s Bastion (Ex)
At 2nd level, the ascendant vanguard’s determination in shaping destiny staves off forces which would guide their action. The ascendant vanguard gains a +4 sacred or profane bonus (chosen when this ability is gained) to saving throws against compulsion and curse effects. At 8th level, the ascendant vanguard becomes immune to compulsion and curse effects.

++ Restore Autonomy (Su)
At 2nd level, the ascendant vanguard gains the ability to remove debilitating effects which impair the thoughts and actions of their allies. A number of times per day equal to their class level, the ascendant vanguard may end the dazed, shaken, or staggered condition on a touched creature or an ally affected by unified in agency as a standard action. The ascendant vanguard may target themself with this ability as a swift action.

At 5th level, the ascendant vanguard may also use this ability to end the confused or frightened conditions. In addition, an affected creature is granted a new saving throw against any ongoing effect they are currently suffering from that normally grants a saving throw using the original DC, even if they have already failed the initial save and would not normally be granted another. Poison and disease effects that can worsen on a failed saving throw do not count this saving throw as a failure when determining such effects if it is unsuccessful.

At 8th level, the ascendant vanguard may also affect any targeted creature with the break enchantment spell, using the ascendant vanguard’s character level as their caster level.

++ Ascension Boon
As an ascendant vanguard gains levels, they gain boons tied to their path of ascension selected with the Dedicate Of Ascension feat. The nature of the boons varies depending on the ascendant vanguard’s chosen path. Each path of ascension grants three boons, each more powerful than the last. At 3rd level, the ascendant vanguard gains the first boon. At 6th level, they gain the second boon, and at 9th level, they gain the third boon. When an ascension boon grants a spell-like ability, the ascendant vanguard’s level for the spell-like ability is equal to their total character level. This ability allows an ascendant vanguard to access these boons earlier than normal; it does not grant additional uses of the boons once the character reaches the necessary Hit Dice to earn the boons normally.

++ Glimpse of Futurity (Su)
At 10th level, the ascendant vanguard gains a perpetual connection to the future they seek to define for themselves. The ascendant vanguard gains fast healing 5. In addition, once per day as an immediate action, the ascendant vanguard may inure themselves against the ravages of the present, becoming immune to all effects and damage until the end of their next turn.

+ Feats

++ Dedicate Of Ascension
**Prerequisite:** Knowledge (history) 3 ranks.

**Benefit:** Choose a path of ascension. Each path of ascension requires a different initial meditation which takes 8 hours. Once you have performed the meditation, you gain the benefit of a special ability or resistance as indicated in the meditation entry for the path of ascension for which you have performed the meditation.

If you have at least 12 Hit Dice, you also gain the first boon granted by your path of ascension. If you have at least 16 Hit Dice, you also gain the second boon. If you have 20 Hit Dice or more, you also gain the third boon. Unless a specific duration or number of uses per day is listed, a boon’s effects are constant. If you have levels in the ascendant vanguard prestige class, you gain access to these boons at lower levels as a benefit of your prestige class.
----
+ Paths of Ascension
Paths of ascension represent unique benefits akin to Deific Obediences gained through the Dedicate Of Ascension feat.

++ Ascension Unto Aggregate
Meditation: Establish a vision of a world without imperfection or conflict, where every being and force occupies a comfortable place within a grand scheme, where all minds understand their interconnectedness and codependence within a unified cosmos.

**Meditation Benefit:** Gain a +1 bonus on all ability checks.

**First Boon:** Architect’s Optimization (Sp): //hermean potential// 3/day, //visualization of the body// 2/day, or //channel vigor// 1/day

**Second Boon:** Guide To Harmony (Su): Whenever you succeed at an attack roll or aid another check against a target, you may grant them either a +2 insight bonus on their next saving throw or cause the target to take a -2 penalty to their next saving throw. These effects are lost at the end of the target’s next turn.

**Third Boon:** Unity of All (Su): Whenever you use an ability that targets two or more creatures, you may target twice as many creatures within range.

++ Ascension Unto Boundless
Meditation: Establish a vision of a world where every phenomenon has an explanation and every problem a solution, where suffering and insufficiency are no longer necessary and no avenues of understanding or control are considered unattainable.

**Meditation Benefit:** For the purpose of calculating the range (but not area) of abilities with a range of close, medium, or long range, double your base attack bonus, caster level, effective skill ranks, and class level.

**First Boon:** Worlds of Possibility (Sp): //sure casting// 3/day, //investigative mind// 2/day, or //channel the gift// 1/day

**Second Boon:** Time Enough At Last (Su): As a move or standard action, you may increase your initiative count on any future turns by up to 5 for the rest of the encounter. You may use this ability any number of times during an encounter. 

**Third Boon:** Fit to My Design (Su): Whenever you use an ability that covers an area, any numeric measurements of the ability’s area increase by 100%. Whenever you use an ability that targets a specific creature or location with a non-instantaneous duration, you may choose to double the duration of the ability. This ability stacks with the Extend Spell and Widen Spell metamagic or metapsionic feats.

++ Ascension Unto Consumption
**Meditation:** Establish a vision of a world that exists beyond yourself and your surroundings, which will emerge and mature long after you have passed, which will take the place of all that you know, love, and dread without any inkling of what came before.

**Meditation Benefit:** Gain a +1 bonus to CMB.

**First Boon:** Unyielding Ruin (Sp): //magic missile// 3/day, //instant weapon// 2/day, or //deadly juggernaut// 1/day

**Second Boon:** Until Nothing Remains (Su): A target who takes damage from one of your attacks, has its equipment damaged by one of your attacks, or fails a saving throw against one of your abilities reduces all temporary hit points and healing they receive by 50% for 1 round. This does not affect healing gained from fast healing or regeneration. A character who succeeds at a DC 20 Heal check recognizes that the effects of their healing would be reduced by this ability.

**Third Boon:** Cataclysmic Advance (Su): Your abilities ignore any damage reduction, energy resistance, or hardness that targets may possess. Whenever you succeed at an attack action against a target, the target must succeed at a Will saving throw (DC 10 + 1/2 your Hit Dice + your highest ability score modifier) or lose one of its defensive abilities for 2d6 rounds. You can choose which defensive ability is lost from the following options: a single immunity, all resistances, regeneration, or spell resistance. Whether or not the target fails this save, it cannot be targeted by this ability removal again (by you or another character) for 24 hours.

++ Ascension Unto Dissonance
**Meditation:** Establish a vision of a world where you are fully vindicated, where all of the beliefs that you have held are proven right for all to witness, where irrefutable evidence exists to justify your ideology beyond any shade of reproach.

**Meditation Benefit:** Reduce the first instance of ability damage, drain, or burn you take each day by 1/2 your character level (minimum 1), to a minimum damage, drain, or burn of 0.

**First Boon:** Seeker of Vindication (Sp): //know the enemy// 3/day, //death knell// 2/day, or //borrow fortune// 1/day

**Second Boon:** Weight of Contempt (Ex): You gain a +2 bonus on damage rolls against any target that has taken damage since the end of your last turn. This damage bonus increases to +4 if the target has taken damage from three or more sources since the end of your last turn (separate attacks are considered different sources).

**Third Boon:** At Any Cost (Su): Whenever you see the result of a d20 roll, you may increase one ability score added to the roll by up half its total for the purpose of determining the result. If you do, you suffer ability burn to that score equal to the granted bonus after the roll is resolved. Ability burn functions as ability score damage, but may not be removed by any means other than 8 hours of resting, which removes all burn.

++ Ascension Unto Fantasmic
**Meditation:** Establish a vision of a world that remembers you with awe and fondness, where your actions have truly fulfilled and embodied a cultural ideal, where the example you have led brings joy and inspiration to those who make tomorrow similarly wondrous.

**Meditation Benefit:** You gain a +1 luck bonus on any reroll of a d20 roll.

**First Boon:** Heroic Aspiration (Sp): //unbreakable heart// 3/day, //blessing of courage and life// 2/day, or //heroism// 1/day

**Second Boon:** Weaver of Tales (Su): Whenever you succeed at an attack roll or saving throw, you may spend an immediate action to grant all allies within close range a +2 morale bonus on the next roll they make of that type until the start of your next turn. This bonus cannot apply against the effect which you successfully saved against.

**Third Boon:** Pierce Plausibility (Su): Whenever you target a creature which would be immune to one of your abilities or damage types, you ignore that immunity unless the target succeeds at a Will save (DC 10 + 1/2 your Hit Dice + your highest ability score modifier). A creature which succeeds at their saving throw against this ability cannot have the same immunity ignored for 24 hours.

++ Ascension Unto Imperative
**Meditation:** Establish a vision of a world where right and wrong are no longer questions but instead verifiable facts, where all creatures are treated in accordance with these principles of virtue, where the greatest heights are achieved through a shared moral understanding.

**Meditation Benefit:** Gain a +2 bonus on Will savinZg throws.

**First Boon:** Seeker of Virtue (Sp): //protection from chaos/evil/good/law// 3/day, //align weapon// 2/day, or //aura sight// 1/day

**Second Boon:** Incisive Deconstruction (Su): Whenever you would succeed at a Will saving throw against a curse, illusion, or mind-affecting effect or would be targeted by such an effect while immune to it, you gain a +4 insight bonus on all d20 rolls made against the creator of the effect or any abilities they possess for 1 round. This bonus does not stack with itself.

**Third Boon:** Armaments of Justice (Su): The enhancement bonuses of any weapons, armor, shields, and implements you wield increase by +2, to a maximum of +6. +6 weapons are considered artifacts for the purpose of overcoming damage reduction and regeneration.

++ Ascension Unto Mechanism
**Meditation:** Establish a vision of a world where death and impermanence are merely optional, where that which is treasured shall never fade away, where one need no longer worry over their own mortality or vulnerability and can put total faith in their own survival.

**Meditation Benefit:** Whenever an effect created as at least a swift action would grant you temporary hit points, you gain 3 additional temporary hit points.

**First Boon:** The Sacrosanct Self (Sp): //abjuring step// 3/day, //false life// 2/day, or //protection from energy// 1/day

**Second Boon:** Engine of Perpetuity (Su): Choose positive or negative energy. You are healed by one and become immune to the other.

**Third Boon:** Steps of Eternity (Ex): You no longer need to eat, drink, sleep, or breathe. You are treated as both a construct and undead creature for the bane weapon special ability, a ranger’s favored enemy bonus, and any other effects that would consider creature type in addition to your usual type. You are immune to two of the following sets of conditions, chosen when you gain this boon:
* Ability damage and ability drain
* Death effects and stunning
* Disease and poison
* Paralysis and sleep effects
