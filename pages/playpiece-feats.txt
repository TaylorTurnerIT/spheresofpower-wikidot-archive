Feats marked as (Playpiece) pertain to physical equipment that the player possesses, such as dice. 

+++ Dice Color Advantage (Combat) (Playpiece)
**Benefit:** Whenever you roll a damage die on an attack or sphere effect, you gain a bonus based on the color of the die. If multiple dice of the same color are rolled, their bonuses are cumulative. If a dice is multiple colors, a single die may count as up to two of its colors for the purpose of this feat’s benefits. A penalty against a specific combat maneuver is not cumulative across multiple attacks or effects (it uses only the greatest penalty). If multiple dice grant damage bonuses against a single target, you may divide this additional damage from different dice among different targets.

**Red:** The target suffers a -1 penalty to their CMD against the next bull rush combat maneuver you attempt against them before the end of your next turn. 
**Orange:** The target suffers a -1 penalty to their CMD against the next disarm combat maneuver you attempt against them before the end of your next turn. 
**Yellow:** The target suffers a -1 penalty to their CMD against the next grapple combat maneuver you attempt against them before the end of your next turn. 
**Green:** The target suffers a -1 penalty to their CMD against the next overrun combat maneuver you attempt against them before the end of your next turn. 
**Blue:** The target suffers a -1 penalty to their CMD against the next sunder combat maneuver you attempt against them before the end of your next turn.
**Purple:** The target suffers a -1 penalty to their CMD against the next trip combat maneuver you attempt against them before the end of your next turn. 
**Pink:** The target suffers a -1 penalty to their CMD against the next drag combat maneuver you attempt against them before the end of your next turn. 
**Black:** The target suffers a -1 penalty to their CMD against the next steal combat maneuver you attempt against them before the end of your next turn. 
**White:** The target suffers a -1 penalty to their CMD against the next reposition combat maneuver you attempt against them before the end of your next turn. 
**Gray:** The target suffers a -1 penalty to their CMD against the next dirty trick combat maneuver you attempt against them before the end of your next turn. 
**Gold:** If you have spent 2 or more spell points this turn, the attack or effect deals 1 additional point of damage to a single target. 
**Silver:** If you have outwitted a target or gained/spent skill leverage this turn, the attack or effect deals 1 additional point of damage to a single target. 
**Bronze:** If you have spent martial focus this turn, the attack or effect deals 1 additional point of damage to a single target. 
**Transparent:** If you have not spent or regained any spell points, martial focus, or skill leverage since the start of your last turn and have not outwitted any targets, the attack or effect deals 1 additional point of damage to a single target.
**Unpainted Numbers/Crayon-drawn Numbers:** If the damage is dealt by a trap or environmental effect, the attack or effect deals 1 additional point of damage to a single target.

+++ Dice Material Advantage (Combat) (Playpiece)
**Benefit:** Whenever you roll a damage die on an attack or sphere effect, you gain a bonus based on the physical material the die is made out of. If multiple dice of the same material are rolled, their bonuses are cumulative. If a dice is made out of multiple materials, a single die may count as up to two of its materials for the purpose of this feat’s benefits.

**Bone/horn:** If the target possesses DR/good or DR/evil, that DR is reduced by 1 against this attack or effect. 
**Ceramic/Clay:** If the target possesses fire resistance, that energy resistance is reduced by 1 against this attack or effect.  
**Digital or Electronic:** If the target possesses electricity resistance, that energy resistance is reduced by 1 against this attack or effect.  
**Glass/Obsidian/Crystal/Silicon:** If the target possesses DR/slashing, that DR is reduced by 1 against this attack or effect. 
**Hollow:** If the target possesses sonic resistance, that energy resistance is reduced by 1 against this attack or effect. 
**Liquid Core:** If the target possesses DR/chaotic or DR/lawful, that DR is reduced by 1 against this attack or effect. 
**Metal:** If the target possesses DR/cold iron, DR/silver, or DR/adamantine, that DR is reduced by 1 against this attack or effect. 
**Plastic:** If the target possesses DR/bludgeoning, that DR is reduced by 1 against this attack or effect. 
**Resin/Candy:** If the target possesses cold resistance, that energy resistance is reduced by 1 against this attack or effect.  
**Stone/Gemstone:** If the target possesses acid resistance, that energy resistance is reduced by 1 against this attack or effect. 
**Wood:** If the target possesses DR/piercing, that DR is reduced by 1 against this attack or effect. 

+++ Dice Shape Advantage (Combat) (Playpiece)
**Benefit:** Whenever you roll a die of the listed shape and add the corresponding bonus type to the roll, you may spend an immediate action to reroll the die after seeing the result. If you do, the corresponding bonus type does not apply to the reroll.
**Faceted Solid Die:** Sacred or Profane bonus 
**Spinner Die:** Luck bonus
**Coin Die:** Competence Bonus
**Bullet/Barrel Die:** Morale Bonus
**Digital Die:** Insight Bonus

+++ Special Dice Advantage (Playpiece)
**Benefit:** Choose up to three specific dice in your possession and assign one of the following properties to each of them. You may assign the same property to multiple dice, but you may only use one die when using any of the abilities from this feat. No die can ever have more than one property from this feat. 

**Class Act:** Select a class feature which requires you to roll one or more dice when used (such as channel energy, sneak attack, or inspiration). You may treat any 1 rolled using the chosen die as part of that class feature as instead rolling a 2.
**Cut, Color, and Die:** Choose a color, material, and shape. For the purpose of feats, the chosen die counts as the chosen color, material, and shape in addition to its normal color, material, and shape.
**Do or Die:** Choose an ability score. In place of adding your relevant ability modifier to ability checks you make with that ability score, you may choose to instead roll the chosen die and add its result to your ability check. If you do, the check no longer counts as an ability check. The average expected result of the chosen die (rounded down) cannot exceed your normal ability score modifier.
**Last Die-ing Breath:** Whenever you fall below 0 hit points, you may roll your chosen die and gain a number of temporary hit points equal to the result. These temporary hit points last for 1 minute. The average expected result of the chosen die (rounded down) cannot exceed twice your character level.
**The Die is Cast:** Whenever you cast a spell or magic sphere effect that lasts for 3 or more rounds, you may roll your chosen die. The duration of the spell or effect is increased by a number of rounds equal to your chosen die’s result minus the chosen die’s average result (rounded down, minimum of 1 round). The average expected result of the chosen die (rounded down) cannot exceed twice your caster level.

**Special:** You may select this feat multiple times, each time selecting three different dice.

+ Playpiece Items

++++ Novelty Dice
**Price:** see text
Though they aren’t typically accepted in high-stakes games, novelty dice are often carried by adventurers as reflections of their personality. While many claim that some otherworldly patron’s possession of such dice is sufficient to fuel certain abilities (such as the Dice Color Specialization feat), those who reject such a patron or wish not to burden them will the procurement of such baubles collect such dice for themselves.

Every novelty die possesses a single shape and material and costs 50 gp. A base die is made of a single material and color, though additional colors and materials can be added to a novelty die at a cost of 10 gp per color and 25 gp per material.
