Those who understand the future have the unique opportunity to seize upon it. Agents of change excel at leveraging their divinatory powers in such a way.

This archetype requires the [[[augur]]] oracle archetype

++ ##4B0092|Trade Tradition##
If this is the character’s first level, the agent of change chooses a trade tradition. Her trade rank is competent. If this is not the character’s first level, she instead chooses one trade talent from the Vocation sphere as a bonus talent. 

This replaces the oracle’s normal class skills. The agent of change gains class skills as normal from her mystery.

++ ##4B0092|Blended Training##
An agent of change gains a magic, combat, or skill talent (some of which are required to be [utility] talents) at the listed levels using the virtuoso talent progression.

This replaces magic talents.

++ ##4B0092|Reader of Omens##
Whenever an augur class feature would allow the agent of change to gain a Divination or Fate sphere talent, the agent of change may choose to gain a Study sphere talent.

++ ##4B0092|Agent's Deeds##
An agent of change may replace any deed they gain from augur archetype with one of the following deeds or may replace any mystery they gain with two of the following deeds provided that she meets the prerequisites.

++++ ##4B0092|Elaborate Weave (Su)##
Whenever the agent of change applies a [complication] talent to a plan, she may spend a point of kismet to apply an additional [complication] talent or automatically succeed on a single skill check added as part of a [complication] talent. She may use this ability any number of times on a single plan as long as she has kismet to spend.

++++ ##4B0092|Preconceived Notions (Su)##
Whenever the agent of change begins a theory, she may spend up to one point of kismet plus an additional point for every 4 oracle levels she possesses to add a number of notions to the theory equal to the amount of kismet spent.

++++ ##4B0092|Forewarned is Forearmed (Su) (Requires oracle level 7th)##
By spending a point of kismet, the agent of change may reveal a plan as a standard action rather than a full-round action. At 13th level, she may instead spend 2 points of kismet to reveal a plan as a move action.

++++ ##4B0092|Miraculous Contingency (Su) (Requires oracle level 11th)##
The oracle gains the magical contingencies mastermind trick as a mastermind of her oracle level. They may use these tricks in conjunction with a spell effect they can create with their agent of change abilities as an alternative to using it with an item. At 15 level, the agent of change may take this revelation again to gain the prepared magical contingency mastermind trick.
